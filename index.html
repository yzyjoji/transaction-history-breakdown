Understanding Your Fees

  • Trading Fees

    These are the fees you pay for executing trades. They are calculated based on your trade size and whether you are a **maker** (placing a limit order that adds liquidity to the market) or a **taker** (placing a market order that immediately fills against an existing order). Taker fees are typically higher than maker fees.

  • Funding Fees

    Funding fees are periodic payments exchanged between long and short traders. They ensure the perpetual contract price stays close to the underlying asset's price. Depending on market conditions, you may either pay or receive funding fees.

  • Liquidation Fees

    A liquidation fee is a penalty incurred when a leveraged position is automatically closed by the exchange. This happens when your margin balance falls below the required maintenance margin level, usually due to a sudden market move against your position.
